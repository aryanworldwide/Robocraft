RoboCraft is made and maintained by a school student in Lucknow, India, as a personal (non-commercial) project.

Get in touch

  • Problems with content or the app: use the Report button inside the app — it tells us the screen you were on. (Settings → Report a problem.)
  • Bugs and feature ideas: open an issue on GitHub.
  • Security reports: see the security page.
  • Everything else, including privacy and data-deletion requests: robocraft.support@example.com.

Data requests

You can delete your account and your synced progress yourself at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. You can also e-mail us and we will complete the deletion within 30 days. See the privacy policy for what is stored and where.
